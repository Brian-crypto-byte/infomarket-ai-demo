Privacy Policy

This draft policy is for product development and legal review. Replace it with counsel-approved policy text before production launch.

1. Data We Collect

We may collect account identifiers, email addresses, wallet addresses, device information, trading activity, ledger events, risk signals, KYC status, and support records.

2. How Data Is Used

Data is used to operate accounts, process orders and withdrawals, provide rewards, manage vault and insurance products, prevent fraud, meet compliance obligations, and improve product reliability.

3. Wallet and Chain Data

Wallet addresses and supported on-chain transactions may be visible on public blockchains. Internal ledger balances remain platform records and are subject to audit and risk review.

4. Retention and Security

Production systems should define retention schedules, encryption, access controls, audit logging, and incident response procedures before launch.

5. User Rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction where applicable law grants those rights, subject to compliance and fraud-prevention requirements.
