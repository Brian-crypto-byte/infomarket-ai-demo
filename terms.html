Legal

User Agreement

These terms describe account access, prediction trading, internal ledger balances, vault plans, insurance cover, and withdrawal review.

1. Eligibility

Users must satisfy applicable age, account security, sanctions, and platform safety requirements before trading or using vault and insurance products.

2. Prediction Trading

Markets are event-based prediction contracts. Football match-result and correct-score markets use YES / NO outcomes. Correct-score positions use fixed odds and cannot be sold before settlement.

3. Internal Ledger

Trading, settlement, rewards, vault accounting, and insurance nodes are maintained in the platform's internal ledger. On-chain operations are limited to supported deposits, withdrawals, and token claims.

4. Risk and Compliance

The platform may reject orders, review withdrawals, or suspend accounts to protect user balances, platform solvency, and account security.

5. No Investment Advice

Market information, odds, and rewards are provided for trading functionality only and are not financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.
